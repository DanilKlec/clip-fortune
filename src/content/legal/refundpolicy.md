## 1\. Overview

Robinzone is a digital service that delivers AI-generated content (images, video, text, and other outputs) and consumable generation credits. Because access and credits are made available to you immediately, purchases are generally non-refundable except as described in this policy or as required by applicable law.

## 2\. Subscriptions

### 2.1. Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. Cancellation stops future renewals; it takes effect at the end of your current billing period, and you retain access until then.

### 2.2. No refunds for partial periods

We do not provide refunds or credits for partial billing periods, unused time, or unused credits remaining at cancellation, except where required by applicable law.

### 2.3. Renewals

Subscription renewals are charged automatically. If you do not wish to renew, cancel before the renewal date. We generally do not refund a renewal charge that occurs because a subscription was not cancelled in time, but we may consider reasonable requests submitted shortly after the charge on a case-by-case basis.

## 3\. Generation Credits and One-Time Purchases

3.1. Generation credits and other one-time purchases are consumable digital goods. Once credits have been used (in whole or in part), the used portion is non-refundable.

3.2. Unused credits may be refundable at our discretion if requested within a reasonable period after purchase and before substantial use, subject to your statutory rights in Section 5.

## 4\. Discretionary and Goodwill Refunds

Even where a refund is not strictly required, we may, at our sole discretion, issue a full or partial refund or credit — for example, in cases of:

- Duplicate or accidental charges;
- A documented technical failure on our side that prevented you from using the Service or consuming credits you paid for;
- A charge you did not authorize (subject to verification).

Refund requests based on dissatisfaction with the quality, style, or accuracy of AI-Generated Content are assessed case-by-case, since AI outputs are inherently variable and provided “as is.”

## 5\. Your Statutory Rights (EU/EEA Consumers)

5.1. If you are a consumer in the EU/EEA, you generally have a 14-day right of withdrawal from distance contracts for digital services.

5.2. **Important — waiver for immediate digital delivery.** Where you request immediate access to the Service or immediate delivery of digital content/credits and acknowledge that you lose your right of withdrawal once performance begins, this 14-day right no longer applies to content already delivered or credits already consumed. By starting to use the Service or consuming credits during the withdrawal period, you provide this express consent and acknowledgement.

5.3. **Note for EU residents:** If you are an EU resident, you have the right to withdraw from the agreement for purchase of digital content without charge and without giving any reason within fourteen (14) days from the date of such agreement conclusion. The withdrawal right does not apply if the performance of the agreement has begun with your prior express consent and your acknowledgement that you thereby lose your right of withdrawal. ## 6\. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email [support@robinzone.ai](mailto:support@robinzone.ai) from the email address associated with your account and include:

- Your account email;
- The date and amount of the charge;
- The order/transaction ID or receipt, if available;
- The reason for your request.

We aim to review and respond to refund requests within a reasonable time, typically within 5–10 business days.

## 7\. How Refunds Are Issued

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the purchase, via our payment processor. The time it takes for the refund to appear depends on your payment provider and is outside our control.

## 8\. Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is incorrect, please contact us first so we can resolve it. Initiating a chargeback without contacting us may result in suspension or termination of your account while the dispute is investigated.
