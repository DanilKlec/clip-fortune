## 1\. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, to make them more efficient, and to provide reporting information. We also use similar technologies such as pixels, tags, software development kits (SDKs), and local storage, which we refer to collectively as “cookies” in this policy.

## 2\. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

- Keep you signed in and maintain your session;
- Remember your preferences and settings (such as language);
- Keep the Service secure and detect fraud or abuse;
- Understand how visitors use the Service so we can improve it;
- Measure and optimize our marketing and advertising campaigns.

## 3\. Types of Cookies We Use

### 3.1. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Service to function. They enable core features such as authentication, session management, security, and load balancing. The Service cannot work properly without them, so they cannot be switched off through our consent tools.

### 3.2. Functional Cookies

These cookies remember choices you make (such as language or interface preferences) to provide a more personalized experience. Disabling them may affect certain features.

### 3.3. Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how users interact with the Service — for example, which features are used, how long sessions last, and where errors occur. We use tools such as Google Analytics for this purpose. The data is generally aggregated and used to improve the Service.

### 3.4. Advertising and Marketing Cookies

These cookies are used to measure the performance of our advertising and, where applicable, to deliver relevant ads and limit how often you see them. We use tools such as Meta/Facebook Pixel, TikTok Pixel, and Google Ads for this purpose. These providers may combine this information with other data they hold.

## 4\. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third parties that provide services to us, including:

- Google (Google Analytics, Google Ads) — analytics and advertising;
- Meta Platforms (Facebook Pixel) — advertising measurement and retargeting;
- TikTok (TikTok Pixel) — advertising measurement and retargeting;
- Our payment processor — fraud prevention and secure checkout.

These third parties are responsible for their own cookies and process data under their own privacy policies. We do not control the cookies set by these third parties.

## 5\. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Where required by applicable law (including in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent before placing non-essential cookies, via our cookie consent banner. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time through the banner or your account settings.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings — most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may cause parts of the Service to stop working. To opt out of certain analytics and advertising cookies, you can also use the tools provided by the relevant third parties (for example, Google Analytics opt-out and your ad preference settings on Meta and TikTok).
